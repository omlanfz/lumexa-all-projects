## Part 2: Service-Idea Brainstorm

For each skill above, brainstorm at least one possible service using the formula: **Skill + Specific Output + Specific Audience + Scope Boundary.** Aim for 2–3 ideas total.

### Example (Jordan R.)

| Skill | Possible Service | Who Needs This |
|---|---|---|
| Building basic websites | Simple 3-page websites for school clubs, delivered in 1 week, 1 revision round included | Student clubs, school teams, small youth groups without a website |
| Slide/presentation design | A matching 10-slide competition or pitch deck, turned around in 3 days | Debate teams, student entrepreneurs, classmates preparing for a presentation |
| Data tracking & charting | A spreadsheet that auto-tracks and charts a club's fundraiser or event signups | Club organizers, class fundraiser leads, event committees |

## Part 3: My Chosen Service

Review your brainstorm. Circle or highlight the idea you can describe most confidently in one sentence. Then complete the template below.

## Self-Check Before You Submit

- [ ] My service names a specific, concrete output (not just a skill area)
- [ ] My service names a specific audience (not "everyone" or "anyone")
- [ ] My service includes a scope boundary (a number, timeframe, or exclusion)
- [ ] My service is based on a skill I have real Course 16 evidence for
- [ ] I could say my final service statement out loud, in one breath, without rereading it
