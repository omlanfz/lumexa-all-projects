## Part 2: Safety Checklist

Check every box honestly before treating any entry above as a real next step.

- [ ] A trusted adult is aware of and involved in every real client conversation I plan to have.
- [ ] I have not listed any strangers, online-only contacts, or people I only know through a marketplace or app.
- [ ] I will not meet anyone in person alone — a parent/guardian or other trusted adult will be present or has approved the meeting.
- [ ] I will not share my home address, school schedule, or personal contact details without a trusted adult's approval.
- [ ] Any money, payment method, or contract detail will be reviewed by a trusted adult before I agree to it.
- [ ] If anything about a conversation or request feels off, I will stop and tell a trusted adult right away.
- [ ] Every entry on my warm-network list is someone I or my family has an actual real-life connection to.
