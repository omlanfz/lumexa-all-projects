# Course 17 — Skills That Pay

**Ages:** 15–18 · **Lessons:** 8 · **Format:** Live, instructor-led

## Purpose
Students learn how a skill becomes a service and a service becomes a professional
opportunity — without assuming every teenager can independently use adult freelance
marketplaces. Real platforms (Fiverr, Etsy, Upwork, Gumroad) are taught as case studies with
their real age/account rules, never as a workaround.

## Tools
Google Docs, Canva, Notion, Email, Spreadsheets.

## Final Deliverables
- A professional service package
- A pricing worksheet
- A proposal + client/project brief
- A full simulated client project

## Lesson Sequence
1. How the Freelance Economy Works
2. Turn a Skill Into a Service
3. Build a Service Package
4. Pricing, Scope, and Agreements
5. Finding Your First Opportunities
6. Marketplace Models: What Changes at Different Ages?
7. Proposals, Communication, and Delivery
8. The Client Simulation

## Non-Negotiable Safety Rules
Lumexa never instructs a student to bypass an age requirement or misrepresent their age. A
generic agreement template is never taught as a legally binding contract. Real paid client
work as a minor should involve a parent/guardian's awareness. All age/account rules for
external platforms are flagged as subject to change and should be verified before teaching.

## Why It Matters
Real opportunities require real professional skills — scoping, pricing, proposing,
delivering. The Client Simulation proves a student can execute all of them, safely, before
ever touching a real client engagement.
