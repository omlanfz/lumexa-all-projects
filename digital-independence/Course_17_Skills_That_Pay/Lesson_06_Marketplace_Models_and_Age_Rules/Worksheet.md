## Worked Example: Jordan R., Age 16

Below is a filled-in example from a fictional student, Jordan R., age 16, thinking through this table. Use it as a model for your own reasoning — your answers don't need to match Jordan's, since your own situation is different.

| Platform | Who can use it & how | What changes at 18 | What I'd need if I wanted to use it responsibly |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fiverr** | 13–17 can use it only through an account owned and managed by a parent or legal guardian. I can't open my own account yet. | I'd be able to open and control my own adult account under full terms. | I'd need to talk to my mom or dad, explain what I want to sell, and ask if they'd be willing to open and own the account with me — and I'd still need to double-check Fiverr's current rules first, since I'm only going off what we studied in class. |
| **Upwork** | Not available to me at all right now — it requires being 18, or the age of majority where I live, which is 18. | I'd be old enough to make my own account and use the platform directly. | Nothing to do right now except wait until I turn 18 (or check if my local age of majority is different) — this one isn't a "workaround" situation, it's just not available to me yet. |
| **Etsy** | Same as Fiverr — 13–17 needs a parent/guardian-owned shop account. I could sell my digital art through it, but not on my own. | I could open and run my own shop account independently. | Same as Fiverr: talk to a guardian about co-owning the account, and confirm Etsy's current policy before doing anything, since class material can be out of date by the time I'd actually act on it. |
| **Gumroad** | Not sure yet — the class lesson said it depends on my country and situation, not one fixed age. | Might not change much for me specifically if my country's rule is already different from 18 — I don't actually know without checking. | I'd need to look up Gumroad's current policy for my own country specifically, and probably still talk to a parent/guardian about payouts, since payment/tax rules for minors can be complicated no matter what the platform says. |

**Jordan's short reflection:** *"Right now, at 16, the honest answer is that most of these options need a parent or guardian involved, or I need to just wait. That's fine — I'd rather do it the right way than risk losing an account or getting in trouble by pretending to be older than I am."*
