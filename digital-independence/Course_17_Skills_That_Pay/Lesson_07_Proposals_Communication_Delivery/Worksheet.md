## Part 2: Client Email Etiquette Checklist

Check each box before you hit send on any client message:

- [ ] I greeted them by name (or a friendly general greeting if I don't have one).
- [ ] I responded within the window I told them to expect (or within 24 hours if I haven't stated one yet).
- [ ] My tone is clear, warm, and concise — not stiff/formal, not texting-casual.
- [ ] I answered their actual question before adding anything else.
- [ ] If I'm asking something, I asked one clear question at a time (not a long list).
- [ ] I did not oversell ("this will be amazing!") — I described what I'll actually do.
- [ ] I reread my message once before sending.
- [ ] I signed off with my name.

---

## Part 3: Delivery / Handoff Checklist

Use this every time you finish a job — this is exactly what you'll be scored on in Lesson 8's Client Simulation.

- [ ] **Deliverable sent** — the finished file(s) are attached or linked, in the format the client needs (e.g., PDF and PNG, not just one).
- [ ] **Instructions included** — a short note on how to use/open/edit the file (e.g., "The PNG is for posting online, the PDF is for printing at full size").
- [ ] **Thank-you note** — a brief, genuine line thanking them for the opportunity.
- [ ] **Invite for feedback** — a line inviting them to ask for small tweaks or share what they think (within the revision limit you agreed to).

## Filled Example: Proposal for "Jordan R."

**Client's ask:** *"Hey! I'm starting a small Etsy shop selling handmade bracelets and I need someone to organize my sales and expenses in a spreadsheet — I have no idea what I'm doing with numbers. Can you help?"*

**Understanding of Need:** You'd like a simple spreadsheet to track your Etsy sales and expenses so you can see how much you're actually making, since you're just getting started with the business side.

**My Approach:** I'll build a Google Sheet with a Sales tab, an Expenses tab, and a Summary tab that automatically calculates your profit. I'll set it up so you can just type in new numbers each week without needing to touch any formulas.

**What's Included:**
- A Google Sheet with 3 tabs: Sales, Expenses, Summary
- Automatic profit calculation on the Summary tab
- Color-coded formatting so it's easy to read
- A short instructions page (or note) explaining how to enter new sales/expenses
- One round of revisions if something doesn't match how you actually track things

**Price:** $20

**Timeline:** First draft within 4 days of you confirming; final version within 2 days after your feedback.

**Next Steps:** Reply "yes" and send me an example of a recent sale and expense (just so I can format the columns correctly), and I'll get started.
