## Part 1: Platform / Model Comparison

| Platform or model | What it's generally used for | Age & account rules (as generally understood — verify before relying on it) | Verified as of (date checked) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fiverr** | Clients post requests or freelancers list fixed-price "gigs" for services like design, writing, video editing, voice work, and more. | Users aged 13–17 may use Fiverr only through an account owned/managed by a parent or legal guardian. Fiverr's terms describe the age floor and the parental-account requirement; check Fiverr's current Terms of Service for exact wording. | ______________ |
| **Upwork** | Larger and longer freelance projects and contracts, often for professional/business clients, across writing, development, design, admin work, and more. | Upwork generally requires users to be 18 or the age of majority in their location; it is not structured for a minor's account, even with a parent's involvement. | ______________ |
| **Etsy** | An online marketplace mainly for selling handmade goods, art, craft supplies, and some digital/print-on-demand products. | Etsy allows users aged 13–17 to have a shop only through a parent or legal guardian's account, with that adult responsible for the shop. Check Etsy's current seller policies for exact terms. | ______________ |
| **Gumroad** | A platform for selling digital products directly — templates, ebooks, music, courses, design assets — without a traditional storefront. | Age and account requirements vary by country and can change; Gumroad's own terms and payment-processor requirements should be checked directly rather than assumed. | ______________ |
| **Direct referrals** (family friends, neighbors, school/community contacts) | Someone you or your family already knows asks you directly to do a specific job (like Mara's logo example) — no platform involved. | No platform account or age gate applies, but a parent/guardian should still be aware of the arrangement, the price, and how payment is handled, especially for anyone under 18. | ______________ |

*Reminder: platform rules can and do change. Before relying on any rule in this table for a real decision, check the platform's current official Terms of Service or Help Center page yourself (with a parent/guardian, if you're a minor).*
