## Part 2: Scope Boundary Checklist

Check off each item once you've clearly defined it. If you can't check a box, your scope isn't specific enough yet.

- [ ] I've listed exactly what deliverables the client will receive (e.g., number of files, file types).
- [ ] I've stated how many revision rounds are included.
- [ ] I've stated a delivery timeframe (a specific number of days).
- [ ] I've stated what is **NOT** included (e.g., "does not include printing" or "does not include more than 2 revisions").
- [ ] I've stated how the client should ask for changes if something is unclear.
- [ ] My scope line avoids vague words like "etc.," "and more," or "whatever you need."

**Filled Example — Jordan R.'s Scope Checklist**

- [x] Deliverables listed: 2 flyer designs, delivered as PNG and PDF files.
- [x] Revision rounds included: 1 round of revisions per flyer.
- [x] Delivery timeframe: within 6 days of starting.
- [x] Not included: does not include printing, does not include a 3rd flyer design.
- [x] How to request changes: client sends notes by email within 2 days of receiving drafts.
- [x] No vague language used.

> **This is an educational agreement template for practicing clear scope communication. It is NOT a legally binding contract.**
> Writing this down and signing it does not create legal obligations. ### Filled Example — Jordan R.

**Project Agreement (Educational Use Only)**

- Service provider (student): Jordan R.
- Client (fictional/simulated for this activity): "Maya's Bake Club" (fictional school club)
- Date: September 14, 2026

**1. What's included (scope):**
Two (2) flyer designs advertising the Bake Club's fall event, delivered as PNG and PDF files, each with one round of revisions included.

**2. What's NOT included:**
Printing of physical flyers; a third flyer design; unlimited revisions.

**3. Price and pricing method:**
$50 (Flat Rate)

**4. Timeline:**
Work will be delivered within 6 days of the start date.

**5. How changes/extra requests will be handled:**
The client will send all revision notes by email within 2 days of receiving the drafts. Requests beyond the one included revision round will be discussed as a separate, additional price.

**6. How communication will happen:**
Email, checked once per day by Jordan.
