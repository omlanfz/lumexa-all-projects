## Filled Example: Jordan R. — Simple Website Package

Use this as a model. Jordan offers simple one-page websites for local small businesses.

**Service Name:** Jordan's Simple Website Package

**What's Included:**
- 1 single-page website with up to 3 sections (example: About, Services, Contact)
- Business name, logo (if provided), and contact information added to the page
- Mobile-friendly layout that works on phones and computers
- 1 round of text/content updates before the site is considered final

**What's Not Included:**
- Domain name purchase or ongoing website hosting fees
- Ongoing website maintenance or updates after final delivery
- Online store or payment/checkout functionality
- Multiple additional pages beyond the 3 included sections

**Deliverable Format:** 1 live website page, delivered as a working link sent by email, plus a shared Google Doc listing the site's content for the business's records

**Turnaround Time:** 5 business days from when the business sends their logo, text content, and contact details

**Revision Policy:** Up to 1 round of revisions included (example: fixing text, swapping an image, or adjusting colors). Additional rounds of revisions, or requests to add new sections, are treated as a new small paid update.

### Quick Self-Check Before You Submit

- [ ] Could a stranger read my Deliverable Format and know exactly what they'd get?
- [ ] Does my "Not Included" list cover at least 2 realistic things a customer might assume?
- [ ] Does my Turnaround Time say what it starts counting from?
- [ ] Is my Revision Policy a specific number — not "unlimited" and not left blank?
- [ ] Have I read the whole package back as if I were the customer?
