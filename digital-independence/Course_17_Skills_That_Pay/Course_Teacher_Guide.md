# Course 17 — Teacher Guide

## Before You Start
- Review each lesson's Instructor_Guide.md in order.
- Verify current age/account policies for Fiverr, Upwork, Etsy, Gumroad, and any other
  platform mentioned in Lessons 1 and 6 before teaching — these change over time and this
  course is explicit that students should check current policy rather than trust a fixed date.
- Read the entire `Lesson_08_Client_Simulation/` package (Instructor Guide + all
  Simulation_Resources) before starting the simulation — you will role-play the fictional
  client, Priya Alvarez, across three sessions.

## Pacing Overview
| Lesson | Suggested duration |
|---|---|
| 1–7 | 60 min each |
| 8 (Client Simulation) | 3 sessions × 60–75 min |

## The Non-Negotiable Rules (repeat these often)
- Never instruct bypassing an age requirement or misrepresenting age.
- Never imply a generic agreement template is a real legal contract.
- Real client work as a minor should involve a parent/guardian.

## What Comes Next
Course 18, "Professional Presence & Opportunity Building," turns the finished service
package and simulated client project into visible, discoverable proof — students should
leave Course 17 with a completed Client Simulation package in hand.
