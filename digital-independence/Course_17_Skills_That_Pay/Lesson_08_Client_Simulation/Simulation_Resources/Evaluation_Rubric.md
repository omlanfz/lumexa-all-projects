# Client Simulation — Evaluation Rubric

| Criterion | Beginner (1) | Developing (2) | Proficient (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery notes | Missing or ignores the call | Present but generic | Reflects specific details from the discovery call |
| Proposal accuracy | Doesn't match requirements | Mostly matches, some gaps | Fully matches discovery + requirements |
| Pricing & scope clarity | No clear scope boundary | Vague boundary | Clear, specific "included / not included" statement |
| Handling the revision | Ignored, or treated as scope creep incorrectly | Made the change but no reasoning documented | Made the change AND correctly explained why it wasn't scope creep |
| Communication tone | Unprofessional or overly casual/stiff | Adequate | Clear, warm, professional throughout |
| Handoff completeness | Missing instructions or features | Mostly complete | Fully complete, non-technical instructions included |

**Scoring:** 6–9 Beginner · 10–14 Developing · 15–18 Proficient
