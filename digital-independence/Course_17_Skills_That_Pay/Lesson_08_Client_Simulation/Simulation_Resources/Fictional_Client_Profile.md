# Fictional Client Profile
*(Educational simulation — not a real person, organization, or transaction.)*

**Client:** Career Compass Club (student-run school club)
**Contact:** Priya Alvarez, Faculty Advisor
**About:** Career Compass Club helps students explore career paths through guest speakers,
job-shadow days, and a small resource library. The club has ~40 active members and meets
weekly.

**Their Problem:** The club currently shares updates through a group chat and paper flyers.
Members keep missing events, and new students don't know the club exists. Priya wants a
simple, single web page the club can point people to.

**Goals:**
- One clear page describing what the club does and how to join
- A list of upcoming events that's easy to update
- A way for interested students to express interest (a simple form or email link)

**Constraints:**
- No budget for paid tools — must use free hosting (ties directly to the Course 16
  GitHub/Vercel deployment skills)
- Priya is not technical and will need simple, non-technical handoff instructions
- Needs a first version within 2 weeks of the initial ask

**Tone:** Friendly, a little busy, appreciative but direct about what she needs.
