# Discovery Call Script (Instructor Role-Play Guide)
*(Instructor plays Priya. Answer student questions using the details below; if a student asks
something not covered, invent a reasonable, consistent answer.)*

## Opening line (say this to start)
"Hi, thanks for reaching out! I'm glad someone might be able to help — go ahead and ask me
whatever you need to get started."

## Answers to likely student questions
- **What should the page include?** "At minimum: what the club is, how to join, and upcoming
  events. A way for people to express interest would be great too — even just an email link."
- **How often do events change?** "Pretty often — every couple of weeks there's something new."
- **Who updates it after you deliver it?** "Probably me, but I'm not technical, so it needs to
  be simple — plain text edits, nothing complicated."
- **What's the timeline?** "I'd love something within two weeks if possible — nothing urgent
  breaking, just want to stop losing people to the group chat."
- **Budget?** "None, unfortunately — has to be free tools."
- **Do you have a logo or colors already?** "Not really — whatever looks clean and simple is fine."
- **Any examples you like?** "Just simple, one-page club sites — nothing fancy."

## Wrap-up line (say this to end the call)
"This is really helpful, thank you! Let me know what you're thinking in terms of a plan and price."
