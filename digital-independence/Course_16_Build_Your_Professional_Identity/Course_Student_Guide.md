# Course 16 — Student Guide: Build Your Professional Identity

## What This Course Is
Over 8 classes, you'll turn what you've already built at Lumexa into real, credible proof of
your skills — a live website with your name on it, a consistent personal brand, real project
case studies, and a professional bio.

## What You'll Walk Away With
- A live portfolio website (with a real URL you can share)
- A personal brand kit
- 2–3 real project case studies
- A professional bio

## How the 8 Lessons Fit Together
1–2: Figure out who you are professionally and what you're best at.
3: Build the site structure.
4–6: Fill it with your real bio, brand, and case studies.
7: Make it live on the internet.
8: Polish it and add real, honest endorsements.

## Ground Rules
Everything you build here should be true. No fake projects, no fake endorsements, no
pretending you have skills or experience you don't. Honest evidence is worth more than an
impressive-sounding exaggeration — and it's the only kind that holds up when someone checks.
