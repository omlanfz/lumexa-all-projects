# Course 16 — Build Your Professional Identity

**Ages:** 15–18 · **Lessons:** 8 · **Format:** Live, instructor-led

## Purpose
Students turn their existing Lumexa projects into honest, credible evidence of their skills —
a live portfolio website, a personal brand kit, 2–3 project case studies, and a professional
bio — before this path moves toward services (Course 17) and visibility (Course 18).

## Tools
HTML, CSS, Vercel, Canva, GitHub, Google Docs.

## Final Deliverables
- A live, deployed portfolio website
- A personal brand kit (color palette, type pairing, simple mark)
- 2–3 real project case studies
- A professional introduction / bio

## Lesson Sequence
1. What Professional Identity Means in the Digital Age
2. Define Your Strengths and Direction
3. Build Your Portfolio Website
4. Tell Your Story Professionally
5. Create Your Personal Brand
6. Turn Projects Into Proof
7. Launch Your Professional Website
8. Build Social Proof

## Why It Matters
Long before a client, mentor, or employer talks to a student, they will look them up. This
course makes sure what's found is real, honest, and impressive — the credibility layer every
later course in this path builds on.
