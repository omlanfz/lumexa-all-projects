# Course 16 — Teacher Guide

## Before You Start
- Review each lesson's Instructor_Guide.md in order — each assumes the prior lesson's
  deliverable exists (e.g. Lesson 4 assumes the Lesson 3 site skeleton is running).
- Have the `Lesson_03_Portfolio_Website/Projects/Portfolio_Website/completed/` reference open
  as your own demo target — never distribute it to students before Lesson 3 is taught, since
  it spoils the exercise.
- Confirm your own GitHub + Vercel accounts are ready before Lesson 7 — sign-up flows change,
  so do a dry run within a week of teaching that lesson.

## Cross-Lesson Consistency Checks
By Lesson 8, every student's portfolio should have: their real name/direction (not starter
placeholder text), a bio (L4), a consistent brand applied (L5), 2–3 real case studies (L4/L6),
and a live URL (L7). Use the Lesson 8 worksheet's final QA checklist as your grading pass.

## Classroom Notes
This course produces real, permanent artifacts (a live website). Remind students at every
lesson: content should be honest — real projects, real skills, no fabricated achievements or
testimonials. This norm carries into Courses 17 and 18.

## What Comes Next
Course 17, "Skills That Pay," turns this portfolio into a priced, sellable service package —
students should leave Course 16 with a deployed site and real case studies in hand.
