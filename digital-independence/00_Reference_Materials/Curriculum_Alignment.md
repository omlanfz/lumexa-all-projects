# Curriculum Alignment — Digital Independence Path

## 1. Understanding of the Official Curriculum

**Ages:** 15–18
**Structure:** 3 courses × 8 lessons = 24 lessons
**Progression:** Identity → Services → Clients → Professional Presence → Opportunities
(explicitly *not* Fiverr → Upwork → LinkedIn)

The path is evidence-driven, not access-driven: it does not assume a student can or should
independently open adult marketplace or professional-network accounts. It teaches the
underlying professional skill first (define your value, package a service, communicate like
a professional, build reputation), and treats real platforms as case studies with their real
age/account rules — never as something to work around.

### Course 16 — Build Your Professional Identity
Turns existing Lumexa projects into honest, credible proof of skill via a live portfolio site,
brand kit, case studies, and a professional bio.
Tools: HTML, CSS, Vercel, Canva, GitHub, Google Docs.
Deliverables: live portfolio website · personal brand kit · 2–3 project case studies ·
professional intro/bio.
Lessons: (1) What professional identity means in the digital age (2) Define your strengths
and direction (3) Build your portfolio website (4) Tell your story professionally (5) Create
your personal brand (6) Turn projects into proof (7) Launch your professional website
(8) Build social proof.

### Course 17 — Skills That Pay
Teaches how a skill becomes a service and a service becomes a professional opportunity —
without assuming marketplace access. Culminates in a full simulated client project.
Tools: Google Docs, Canva, Notion, Email, Spreadsheets.
Deliverables: professional service package · pricing worksheet · proposal + client/project
brief · full simulated client project.
Lessons: (1) How the freelance economy works (2) Turn a skill into a service (3) Build a
service package (4) Pricing, scope, and agreements (5) Finding your first opportunities
(6) Marketplace models: what changes at different ages? (7) Proposals, communication, and
delivery (8) The client simulation.

### Course 18 — Professional Presence & Opportunity Building
Builds reputation and communication skill using LinkedIn where age-eligible, and an
equally credible portfolio-first alternative where not.
Tools: LinkedIn (age-eligible), Canva, Google Docs, content-planning tools.
Deliverables: professional profile/presence · 5 pieces of professional content · networking
plan · 90-day growth plan.
Lessons: (1) Your professional presence (2) Build your professional profile (3) Showcase
your work (4) Share what you know (5) Build a professional presence beyond LinkedIn
(6) Networking and professional communication (7) Build your 90-day opportunity plan
(8) Measure, reflect, and improve.

## 2. How the Three Courses Connect
Course 16 produces the *evidence* (portfolio, case studies). Course 17 turns that evidence
into a *sellable, priced service* and proves the student can execute a full client engagement
safely, in simulation. Course 18 makes that evidence and service *discoverable* and turns one
finished project into an ongoing pipeline of opportunity, via reputation and communication.
No later course requires an adult platform account the student does not already legally hold.

## 3. Age-Safety Rules Applied Throughout
- Never instructs falsifying age, bypassing verification, or using another person's account.
- Platform mentions (Fiverr, Upwork, Etsy, Gumroad, LinkedIn) always paired with their real
  age/account rules, flagged as subject to change/local law, and taught as case studies.
- LinkedIn requires 16+ (subject to local law/parental consent rules) — students below the
  applicable age use the Course 18 portfolio-first alternative instead of creating an account.
- All client work is simulated and clearly labeled fictional/educational; no real payments,
  real clients, or real contracts are used or implied to be legally binding.

## 4. Assumptions Made (flagged for your review)
- Domain/hosting: Vercel's free tier is assumed for lesson 7 (no purchase required).
- "GitHub" use is scoped to creating a public repo and pushing code — not collaboration
  workflows (PRs, branching) which are out of scope for this path.
- Where the official PDF doesn't specify exact worksheet formats, I designed original
  templates consistent with the stated deliverables.
- Space-themed visual identity is an original design (not reproduced official brand assets).
