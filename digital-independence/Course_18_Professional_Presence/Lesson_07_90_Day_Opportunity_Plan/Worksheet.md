Use everything you've built in Lessons 1–6 (your presence, content pieces, channels, and networking contacts) to fill in your plan below. Be specific — every goal should have a rough date and a way to prove you did it.

## Check-In Reminders

Set a recurring reminder (phone calendar, planner) to check your plan against reality at these points:

- **Day 30 check-in:** Did I complete Month 1's goals? What needs to move to Month 2?
- **Day 60 check-in:** Did I complete Month 2's goals? Is my content cadence still on pace (roughly 1 piece every 2–3 weeks)?
- **Day 90 check-in:** Did I reach 5 total content pieces? Did I complete my opportunity goal(s)? What's my next 90-day plan going to focus on?

Remember: this plan is a default, not a lock. If a real opportunity shows up that isn't on this sheet, you can still take it — just update the plan afterward so it stays useful.
