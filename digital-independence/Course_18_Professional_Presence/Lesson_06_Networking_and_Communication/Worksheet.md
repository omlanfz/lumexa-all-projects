## Part 1: Safety Checklist (Complete This First)

Check each box before you write your message.

- [ ] I am reaching out to someone I already know, or someone one warm introduction away — **not a stranger**.
- [ ] I have identified a trusted adult (parent, guardian, or instructor) who will see my message before I send it.
- [ ] My message does not include my home address, school schedule, passwords, or financial information.
- [ ] I understand that if this person ever suggests meeting in person, that only happens with a trusted adult involved in planning it.
- [ ] If I'm using LinkedIn, I am 16 or older; if not, I'm using my portfolio/PDF resume + email instead.
- [ ] I understand that if a reply ever feels strange, pushy, or uncomfortable, I will stop responding and tell a trusted adult right away.
