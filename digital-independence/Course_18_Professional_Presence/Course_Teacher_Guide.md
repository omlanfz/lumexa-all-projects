# Course 18 — Teacher Guide

## Before You Start
- Determine which students are LinkedIn-eligible (16+, subject to local law) before Lesson 1,
  and verify LinkedIn's current age policy — it should not be assumed fixed.
- Every lesson is taught as two parallel tracks: LinkedIn-eligible and portfolio-first. Never
  treat portfolio-first as a lesser option — frame it as equally credible throughout.
- This is the final course in the 24-lesson path — Lesson 8 closes out the entire Digital
  Independence Path, not just this course.

## Pacing Overview
| Lesson | Suggested duration |
|---|---|
| 1–8 | 60 min each |

## Cross-Course Dependency
This course assumes a completed Course 16 portfolio (for the portfolio-first track and the
Showcase lesson) and a completed Course 17 service package/simulation (referenced as content
and case-study material in Lessons 3–4).

## What Comes Next
Nothing — this is the final lesson of the path. Lesson 8's close should feel like a genuine
capstone: students hand off ownership of their 90-day plan and keep executing independently.
