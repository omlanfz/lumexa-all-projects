### Part 2: Reflection (Jordan's example)

**Most proud of:** "Finishing the client simulation, especially the round where the fake client suddenly wanted extra features. I didn't panic — I asked for a new deadline and explained why, instead of just agreeing to everything for free. That felt like something a real freelancer would do."

**What I'd improve:** "I'd start my content pieces earlier instead of stacking three of them in the last two weeks. The rushed ones are noticeably weaker than the first two I had more time on."

**What changed:** "Before Course 16 I thought 'professional' meant having a fancy resume. Now I think it means being able to explain clearly what you do, show real proof you've done it, and follow through when things get harder than planned. I didn't believe I could offer an actual service before this — now I have a whole package built, and I've practiced defending my own pricing to a difficult client."
