# Course 18 — Professional Presence & Opportunity Building

**Ages:** 15–18 · **Lessons:** 8 · **Format:** Live, instructor-led

## Purpose
Students build reputation and communicate professionally, using LinkedIn where age-eligible
(16+, subject to local law/consent requirements — verify current policy) and an equally
credible portfolio-first alternative where not. Students below the applicable age do not
need to create a LinkedIn account.

## Tools
LinkedIn (where age-eligible), Canva, Google Docs, content-planning tools.

## Final Deliverables
- A professional profile / presence
- 5 pieces of professional content
- A networking plan
- A 90-day growth plan

## Lesson Sequence
1. Your Professional Presence
2. Build Your Professional Profile
3. Showcase Your Work
4. Share What You Know
5. Build a Professional Presence Beyond LinkedIn
6. Networking and Professional Communication
7. Build Your 90-Day Opportunity Plan
8. Measure, Reflect, and Improve

## Why It Matters
A portfolio and a finished project only get discovered if someone is looking. Consistent,
honest, credible visibility compounds over time — turning one finished project into an
ongoing stream of opportunities.
