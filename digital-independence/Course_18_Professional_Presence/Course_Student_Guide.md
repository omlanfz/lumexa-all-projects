# Course 18 — Student Guide: Professional Presence & Opportunity Building

## What This Course Is
Over 8 classes, you'll build a discoverable, credible professional presence — using LinkedIn
if you're 16+ (subject to local law), or an equally strong portfolio-first path if you're
not — and a 90-day plan to keep generating opportunities after the course ends.

## What You'll Walk Away With
- A professional profile / presence
- 5 pieces of professional content
- A networking plan
- A 90-day growth plan

## The Two Paths
If you're 16+ and eligible under your local laws, you'll build this on LinkedIn. If you're
not, you'll build an equally credible version using your Course 16 portfolio site — this is
not a smaller or lesser version, just a different channel.

## How the 8 Lessons Fit Together
1–2: Understand professional presence and build your profile/presence page.
3–4: Showcase real work and share what you know.
5–6: Strengthen your presence beyond one platform, and network safely and professionally.
7–8: Build (and take ownership of) your 90-day plan, and reflect on the whole path.
