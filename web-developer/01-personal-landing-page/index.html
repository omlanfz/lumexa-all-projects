About Me

I started coding at 14 by editing my class's science-fair website, and I haven't stopped since. I'm currently a student focused on HTML, CSS, and JavaScript fundamentals, and I care a lot about building things that work well for everyone — fast load times, readable typography, and layouts that hold up on any screen size.

Outside of code, I run the astronomy club at my school, where I help other students build simple star-tracking pages. I'm looking for opportunities to keep learning, collaborate on real projects, and eventually build software that makes space science more approachable for young people.
