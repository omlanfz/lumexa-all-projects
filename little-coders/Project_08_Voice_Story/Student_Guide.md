# Student Guide — Project 08: Voice-Activated Story

Join Luna the fox on an adventure YOU help control! 🚀

## How to play
1. Open `project.sb3` and click the green flag.
2. Read the story as it appears above Luna and the Narrator.
3. When asked to choose, press **1** to say "LEFT" or **2** to say "RIGHT".
4. Watch your choice change the whole rest of the story!

## Try both paths
Play through once choosing LEFT, then play again choosing RIGHT. The scenes AND the ending are different each time!

## Explore the code
Open the Narrator sprite's scripts. Find the broadcasts named `SaidLeft`, `SaidRight`, `EndingBrave`, and `EndingHappy` — trace how they connect the story together.

🤖 **Robot Tip:** Pressing 1 or 2 stands in for really SAYING "left" or "right" out loud. Check `AI_Training_Guide.md` if you want to train a real voice model to control the story with your actual voice!
