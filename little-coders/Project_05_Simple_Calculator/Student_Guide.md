# ➕ Student Guide: Simple Calculator

## What You're Building

A real calculator! It asks you for two numbers and what to do with them (+, -, *, or /), gives you the answer, and lets you do as many calculations as you want.

## How to Run It

1. Open `simple_calculator.py` in Thonny.
2. Press the green Run button.
3. Type your first number, press Enter.
4. Type your second number, press Enter.
5. Type an operation: `+`, `-`, `*`, or `/`.
6. See your answer!
7. Type "yes" to calculate again, or "no" to stop. You can also type "quit" at the very first question to stop right away.

## Try This

- Try dividing a number by 0. What happens? (It won't crash — it gives you a friendly message instead!)
- Try typing letters instead of a number. What happens? (Same thing — a friendly message, no crash!)
- Try all four operations (+, -, *, /) with the same two numbers and compare the answers.

## Cool Trick Inside

This calculator has a "safety net" built in using something called `try`/`except`. Think of it like a net under a trapeze artist at the circus — if something goes wrong (like dividing by zero), the safety net catches it gently instead of letting the whole program crash!
