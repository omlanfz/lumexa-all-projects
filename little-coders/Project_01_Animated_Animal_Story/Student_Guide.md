# 🚀 Student Guide — Pip the Bunny's Big Adventure

## Welcome!

Open `project.sb3` in Scratch and click the green flag to watch Pip's whole adventure unfold — meeting a fox, crossing a big river, and finding new friends!

## What to Look For

- 🐇 **Pip's hop animation** — watch closely, Pip's legs switch between two costumes really fast to look like hopping!
- 🦊 **The Fox** — notice it was hidden the whole time, until a broadcast message told it to appear.
- 🐢 **The Turtle to the rescue** — it waits quietly until the river scene starts, then swims in to help.
- 🎬 **Three scenes** — meadow, river, and home — all connected using broadcasts, just like in Lesson 4 and Lesson 7!

## 🎨 Make It Yours

Try remixing this project:
1. Change one of Pip's `say` messages to your own words.
2. Add a fourth costume to Pip so it animates even smoother.
3. Add a brand-new animal sprite that shows up in the "home" scene to celebrate with Pip.

## 🧠 Think Like a Coder

Look at the broadcast chain: `fox_intro` → `fox_greeted` → `scene2_river` → `turtle_helped` → `scene3_home`. Each one is like a page turn in the story. Can you trace which sprite sends each broadcast, and which sprite(s) receive it?

## 🏆 Victory Moment

If you can explain how ONE broadcast makes TWO different sprites react at the same time, you've mastered one of the coolest ideas in this whole course!
