# Student Guide — Project 07: AI Rock-Paper-Scissors

Get ready to play Rock-Paper-Scissors against the computer! 🚀

## How to play
1. Open `project.sb3` and click the green flag.
2. Press **1** for Rock, **2** for Paper, **3** for Scissors (or click the sprite).
3. The computer picks its move randomly.
4. Watch who wins the round — your Score goes up when you win!

## Explore the code
- Click the **Referee** sprite and look at its scripts.
- Find the block that makes the computer choose randomly.
- Find the if/else chain that decides the winner.

🤖 **Robot Tip:** Right now your "gesture" is really just a key press — that's a stand-in for a real webcam that could one day SEE your hand shape! Check `AI_Training_Guide.md` if you want to try training that for real.

## Try This
- Play until you get 3 wins in a row.
- Remix the game: change a sound, a message, or add your own backdrop.
