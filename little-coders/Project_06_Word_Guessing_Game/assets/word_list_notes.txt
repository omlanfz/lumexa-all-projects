Word Guessing Game - Secret Word List
======================================
The following 10 kid-friendly words are used as the secret word list in
word_guessing_game.py. All are common, age-appropriate words for ages 6-11.

1. python
2. rocket
3. planet
4. galaxy
5. wizard
6. dragon
7. castle
8. puzzle
9. rainbow
10. volcano

Teachers and students can add more words to the list in word_guessing_game.py
by following the same format: put the new word in lowercase letters, inside
quotes, separated by a comma.
